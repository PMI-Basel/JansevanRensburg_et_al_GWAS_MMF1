This is the code and raw data required to reproduce the figures from the manuscript of Janse van Rensburg xxx. 

The repository is structured according to the different sections of the manuscript:
1: The phenotypic exploration of the accessions grown on the two soil microbiomes.
2: The exploration of GWA peaks, and extraction of genes for further analysis.
3: MBOA sensitivity test of different accessions from the GWAS.
4: The screening of mmf1 mutants, and mutants affected in the ETI signalling pathway for growth feedbacks on conditioned soil.
5: Microbiota analysis of Col-0 (WT) and mmf1 mutants
6: Analysis of the root transcriptome of Col-0 (WT) and mmf1 mutant plants.

Each folder contains the nex^cessary "Input" files, and the annotated R code to reproduce the graphs in the manuscript.
